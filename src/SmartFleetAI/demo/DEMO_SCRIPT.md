# 3-Minute Demo Script

## 1. Opening — 20 seconds
“Supply-chain disruptions can create delays while some vehicles remain underutilised. SmartFleet AI helps a logistics manager understand the situation and choose a practical next action.”

## 2. Dashboard — 30 seconds
Show the KPI cards and utilisation bars.
Say:
“This dashboard shows total vehicles, available vehicles, delayed vehicles and underutilised vehicles.”

## 3. Disruption — 30 seconds
Open Disruptions.
Say:
“Here we can see an Ahmedabad-Surat road disruption with high severity and an expected 2.5-hour delay.”

## 4. AI Recommendation — 50 seconds
Return to Dashboard and click Generate AI Recommendation.
Say:
“The system checks affected deliveries, available vehicles, remaining capacity and utilisation. It recommends a suitable vehicle and explains why.”

## 5. Optimisation — 30 seconds
Open AI Optimisation, select a delivery and click Optimise Assignment.
Say:
“This makes the decision process transparent instead of only showing a black-box answer.”

## 6. AI Assistant — 30 seconds
Ask:
“Which vehicles are underutilised?”
Then:
“Which deliveries are affected?”
Say:
“The assistant gives quick answers from the current logistics data.”

## 7. Closing — 20 seconds
“SmartFleet AI turns disruption and fleet data into an actionable decision, helping logistics teams respond faster and use vehicle capacity more effectively.”
