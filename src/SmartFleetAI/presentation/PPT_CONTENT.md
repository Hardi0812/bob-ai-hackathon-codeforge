## Slide 1 — Title
SmartFleet AI
Supply Chain Disruption Assistant & Fleet Utilisation Optimizer
IBM BoB AI Innovation Hackathon 2026

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Supply-chain disruptions cause delivery delays.
- Vehicle breakdowns and route problems require quick decisions.
- Some vehicles may remain underutilised.
- Manual allocation can be slow.

## Slide 3 — Proposed Solution
SmartFleet AI:
- Monitors fleet utilisation
- Identifies disruption impact
- Finds affected deliveries
- Recommends suitable vehicles
- Provides an AI assistant

## Slide 4 — How It Works
Fleet Data + Delivery Data + Disruption Data
→ Analysis
→ Affected Delivery Detection
→ Vehicle Capacity & Utilisation Check
→ AI Recommendation
→ Decision Support

## Slide 5 — AI Features
- Utilisation analysis
- Disruption impact analysis
- Vehicle reassignment recommendation
- Explainable reasons
- Natural-language assistant
